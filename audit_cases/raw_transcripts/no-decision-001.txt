Meeting: Q2 Product Planning
Date: January 8, 2025

Alice Chen (Product Manager) opened the meeting and introduced the main agenda item: a discussion about whether to adopt Kubernetes for container orchestration.

Bob Martinez (Engineering Lead) presented the case for one approach, noting the performance benefits and team familiarity. Alice Chen countered with concerns about cost, timeline, and migration risk.

After 40 minutes of discussion, neither side was able to reach a consensus. Alice Chen summarised the situation: "Let's table this until we have more data."

Bob Martinez agreed, adding that they would gather more metrics over the next two weeks before reconvening. Both committed to preparing a written recommendation for the follow-up meeting.

The meeting ended with no formal decision recorded on the Kubernetes vs Docker Swarm decision.
