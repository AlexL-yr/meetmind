Meeting: API Design Session
Date: May 22, 2025

James Cooper (QA Lead) opened the meeting and introduced the main agenda item: a discussion about whether to hire a dedicated DevOps engineer or upskill internally.

Karen White (Scrum Master) presented the case for one approach, noting the performance benefits and team familiarity. James Cooper countered with concerns about cost, timeline, and migration risk.

After 40 minutes of discussion, neither side was able to reach a consensus. James Cooper summarised the situation: "We'll need more information before committing to a direction."

Karen White agreed, adding that they would gather more metrics over the next two weeks before reconvening. Both committed to preparing a written recommendation for the follow-up meeting.

The meeting ended with no formal decision recorded on the hiring vs upskilling decision.
