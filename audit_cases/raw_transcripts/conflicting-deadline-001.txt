Meeting: Q2 Product Planning
Date: January 8, 2025

Alice Chen (Product Manager) opened by reviewing the timeline for the database migration project, which is critical for the upcoming platform upgrade.

Bob Martinez (Engineering Lead) confirmed the migration had been scoped and the initial estimate was end of Q1. This was recorded in the project plan at the start of the quarter.

Later in the meeting, while discussing resource constraints, Alice Chen said: "Given the team's current capacity and the two vacancies we're trying to fill, I think realistically we're looking at mid-May for the full migration to be complete."

Bob Martinez acknowledged the revised estimate but expressed concern about downstream dependencies. No resolution was reached and the conflicting timelines were not reconciled before the meeting ended.

The meeting closed without a confirmed deadline for the database migration.
