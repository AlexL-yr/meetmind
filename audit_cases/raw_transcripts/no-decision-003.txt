Meeting: Infrastructure Migration Review
Date: March 5, 2025

Frank Thompson (VP Sales) opened the meeting and introduced the main agenda item: a discussion about whether to switch from REST to GraphQL for the public API.

Grace Liu (Marketing Director) presented the case for one approach, noting the performance benefits and team familiarity. Frank Thompson countered with concerns about cost, timeline, and migration risk.

After 40 minutes of discussion, neither side was able to reach a consensus. Frank Thompson summarised the situation: "We explicitly agreed not to make this call yet."

Grace Liu agreed, adding that they would gather more metrics over the next two weeks before reconvening. Both committed to preparing a written recommendation for the follow-up meeting.

The meeting ended with no formal decision recorded on the API design decision.
