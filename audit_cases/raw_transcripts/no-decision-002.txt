Meeting: Sprint 15 Retrospective
Date: February 12, 2025

David Kim (CTO) opened the meeting and introduced the main agenda item: a discussion about whether to migrate the monolith to microservices.

Emma Wilson (Design Lead) presented the case for one approach, noting the performance benefits and team familiarity. David Kim countered with concerns about cost, timeline, and migration risk.

After 40 minutes of discussion, neither side was able to reach a consensus. David Kim summarised the situation: "We're not ready to decide today — let's revisit next sprint."

Emma Wilson agreed, adding that they would gather more metrics over the next two weeks before reconvening. Both committed to preparing a written recommendation for the follow-up meeting.

The meeting ended with no formal decision recorded on the monolith migration decision.
