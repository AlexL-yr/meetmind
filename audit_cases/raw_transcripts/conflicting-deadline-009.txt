Meeting: Security Audit Review
Date: September 9, 2025

Ryan Anderson (Product Lead) opened by reviewing the timeline for the database migration project, which is critical for the upcoming platform upgrade.

Sofia Martinez (UX Lead) confirmed the migration had been scoped and the initial estimate was within 10 business days. This was recorded in the project plan at the start of the quarter.

Later in the meeting, while discussing resource constraints, Ryan Anderson said: "Given the team's current capacity and the two vacancies we're trying to fill, I think realistically we're looking at by year-end for the full migration to be complete."

Sofia Martinez acknowledged the revised estimate but expressed concern about downstream dependencies. No resolution was reached and the conflicting timelines were not reconciled before the meeting ended.

The meeting closed without a confirmed deadline for the database migration.
