Meeting: Budget Allocation Meeting
Date: June 3, 2025

Liam Brown (Data Scientist) opened by reviewing the timeline for the database migration project, which is critical for the upcoming platform upgrade.

Maria Garcia (ML Engineer) confirmed the migration had been scoped and the initial estimate was before the board meeting on Thursday. This was recorded in the project plan at the start of the quarter.

Later in the meeting, while discussing resource constraints, Liam Brown said: "Given the team's current capacity and the two vacancies we're trying to fill, I think realistically we're looking at sometime in Q3 for the full migration to be complete."

Maria Garcia acknowledged the revised estimate but expressed concern about downstream dependencies. No resolution was reached and the conflicting timelines were not reconciled before the meeting ended.

The meeting closed without a confirmed deadline for the database migration.
