Meeting: Sprint 15 Retrospective
Date: February 12, 2025

David Kim (CTO) opened by reviewing the timeline for the database migration project, which is critical for the upcoming platform upgrade.

Emma Wilson (Design Lead) confirmed the migration had been scoped and the initial estimate was by March 31st. This was recorded in the project plan at the start of the quarter.

Later in the meeting, while discussing resource constraints, David Kim said: "Given the team's current capacity and the two vacancies we're trying to fill, I think realistically we're looking at before the summer break for the full migration to be complete."

Emma Wilson acknowledged the revised estimate but expressed concern about downstream dependencies. No resolution was reached and the conflicting timelines were not reconciled before the meeting ended.

The meeting closed without a confirmed deadline for the database migration.
